SBIX Certify provides blockchain-anchored proof of creation for digital artists and NFT creators. 

The problem: Art theft before minting. Someone downloads your art, mints it first, and on-chain they look like the original creator. You have no proof you made it first.

The solution: Certify your work BEFORE you mint. SBIX Certify creates a SHA-256 hash, adds a legal RFC-3161 timestamp, and anchors the proof on Tezos blockchain. Your certificate is court-admissible evidence that you created the work on that date.

Features: 60-second certification, legal timestamps (RFC-3161), Tezos + Aleph.im dual anchoring, Ed25519 signatures, professional PDF certificates with QR codes, public verification portal. Free tier (5/month) and Pro plan ($29/month unlimited).

Built for the Tezos creator ecosystem. Complements existing tools like Objkt (minting) and Porcupin (preservation) by protecting the CREATION stage.
